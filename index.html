What We Teach

  • Drum Kit — Groove, fills, independence and reading — from first stick grip to full-kit fluency.
  • Snare & Rudiments — Rudimental technique, sight-reading and audition prep for snare drummers.
  • Marching Percussion — Battery and front ensemble fundamentals for marching band and drumline.
  • Mallet Percussion — Xylophone, marimba and vibraphone technique for concert and jazz players.
